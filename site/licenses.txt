# Third-party notices

mbui itself is MIT-licensed (`LICENSE`, Copyright (c) 2026 still.fail
contributors). It includes, or is derived from, the following works of others,
which keep their own licences. Where a licence below is MIT, its full text is

## Unicode Character Database and conformance tests

- What: the property tables in `src/text/tables.mbt` and
  `src/text/bidi_tables.mbt` (generated by `scripts/gen_unicode.py` and
  `scripts/gen_bidi.py` from the UCD 18.0.0), and the test data embedded in
  `src/text/grapheme_conformance_test.mbt` (all of `GraphemeBreakTest.txt`),
  `src/text/linebreak_conformance_test.mbt` (all of `LineBreakTest.txt`) and
  `src/text/bidi_conformance_test.mbt` (a sample of `BidiCharacterTest.txt`).
- From: https://www.unicode.org/Public/18.0.0/ucd, Unicode, Inc.
- Licence: Unicode License v3 (https://www.unicode.org/license.txt).
  Copyright © 1991-2025 Unicode, Inc.

## MoonBit core's math (FreeBSD msun, musl)

- What: `src/dmath/` (sin, cos, tan, the inverse functions, exp, ln, pow and
  their helpers): MoonBit core's own MoonBit ports (its `math` package's
  non-JS files, moonbit 0.1.20261016), taken whole so that they run on every
  target, js too, in place of the engine's `Math.*`.
- From: https://github.com/moonbitlang/core (`math/`), itself after FreeBSD's
  msun (Sun Microsystems' fdlibm) and musl.
- Licence: Apache License 2.0 (each file's header). Copyright 2025
  International Digital Economy Academy; fdlibm: Copyright (C) 1993 by Sun
  Microsystems, Inc., freely usable with its notice kept.

## Yoga

- What: the flexbox tests `src/flex/yoga_*_test.mbt`, generated by
  `scripts/gen_yoga_tests.py` from Yoga's generated layout tests
  (`javascript/tests/generated/YG*Test.test.ts`, commit
  `52f6d0bd3824296b8e852cb4fcdf3b9821ab2aa5`), and `src/flex/helpers_test.mbt`'s
  measure function after Yoga's `javascript/tests/tools/utils.ts`.
- From: https://github.com/facebook/yoga
- Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates.

## Motion

- What: the easing functions, utilities, generators (spring, inertia,
  keyframes) and transitions in `src/motion` (`easing.mbt`, `utils.mbt`,
  `generators.mbt`, `transition.mbt`) and their tests
  (`motion_port_test.mbt`, from Motion at `ce1d082`), ported from Motion's
  TypeScript.
- From: https://github.com/motiondivision/motion (motion.dev)
- Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) 2024 Motion B.V.

## Jetpack Compose and the Android Open Source Project

- What: `src/ui/scroll.mbt` (and the expected values in
  `src/ui/scroll_wbtest.mbt`, `scripts/scrollplot.py`): Compose's
  `VelocityTracker1D` (Lsq2, `polyFitLeastSquares`), `FlingCalculator` and
  `AndroidFlingSpline` (the `OverScroller` spline), `android.widget.EdgeEffect`'s
  stretch, HWUI's `StretchEffect` and Compose's
  `AndroidEdgeEffectOverscrollEffect`, reimplemented in MoonBit;
  `src/motion/spec.mbt` after Compose's `SpringSimulation`,
  `SpringEstimation.kt` and its easing curves. `scripts/ComposeRef.java` and
  `scripts/compose_ref.sh` run Compose's own classes from Gradle's cache (not
  included) to print reference numbers.
- From: https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/support and
  https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base
- Licence: Apache License 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
  Copyright The Android Open Source Project.

## nanoui's hill-climb benchmark

- What: the scenes of `src/bench` (`scenes.mbt`) and of the Flutter

- From: https://github.com/justrach/nanoui
- Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) 2026 Rach Pradhan.

- Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) 2026 MyGo contributors.
