# ScreenReplay 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

## 中文

ScreenReplay 在你的电脑后台持续录屏，让你可以倒回去保存刚才发生的内容。

**录下了什么**：主屏幕的画面；系统声音（可在设置中关闭）；麦克风（默认关闭）；前台应用的名称、图标和描述；窗口的位置、层叠和标题（用于按窗口导出）；鼠标、键盘和手柄用得多频繁（每 0.1 秒一个强度，只记次数，不记按了哪个键、点在哪里；用于在时间线上标出忙碌的时段）。

**存在哪里**：所有录像和上述记录只保存在你这台电脑上：默认在这台电脑的应用数据目录里，也可以在「设置 › 存储」里改到你选的文件夹；你保存的片段在你选的文件夹里。超过保留时长或超出占用上限的录像会自动删除。ScreenReplay **不会上传**任何录像、截图、窗口标题、应用名或文件。

**使用统计和崩溃报告**：本版本不收集、不发送任何使用统计或崩溃报告，也不连接任何统计服务。将来如果加入（会是默认关闭、由你主动打开），会先更新本政策和日期。

**你的控制**：随时可以在托盘菜单暂停录制，在设置中关闭后台录制、系统声音和麦克风，或在「设置 › 存储」里删除全部录像。

## English

ScreenReplay keeps recording your screen in the background so you can rewind and keep what just happened.

**What it records**: your main screen; system audio (can be turned off in Settings); the microphone (off by default); the name, icon and description of the app in front; windows' positions, stacking and titles (for exporting a single window); how often the mouse, keyboard and game controller are used (one intensity every 0.1 s, counts only, never which keys, buttons or where; to mark the busy stretches on the timeline).

**Where it is kept**: all recordings and the records above stay on this PC only: by default in this PC's app data folder, or in a folder you choose in Settings › Storage; the clips you save go to the folder you choose. Recordings older than your retention time, or beyond your storage limit, are deleted automatically. ScreenReplay **never uploads** recordings, screenshots, window titles, app names or files.

**Usage statistics and crash reports**: this version collects and sends no usage statistics and no crash reports, and connects to no statistics service. If they are added later (off by default, turned on only by you), this policy and its date will be updated first.

**Your control**: pause recording from the tray menu at any time; turn off background recording, system audio and the microphone in Settings; or delete all recordings in Settings › Storage.
